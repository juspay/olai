## What happened, in plain words

Handed a PR instead of a codebase, the room found that the PR's author had
already cut the headline socket — `Query.Recall`, declared in `@olai/ops` and
implemented nowhere in it — and the debate's yield inverted: protect what the
PR got right, install the one receptacle it only named, and catch what the
merge would break. The self-correction culture peaked: **every debater killed
at least one of their own candidates on a test, in public** — fable her own
altitude's package in her opening, opencode both of his named candidates,
grok his entire upstream slate plus two round-1 claims the code contradicted.
And the closing round itself produced a convergence: opencode's closing
conceded the one wall the other two had filed as the edition's open position.

## Ratified (pending the human's word)

1. **`@olai/child` — the child-lifetime receptacle, a workspace leaf.** Three
   copies of subprocess plumbing exist (`chat/src/pipes.ts`, `@kolu/detect`'s
   private re-type, `recall/embedder.ts`) and the third is *wrong* — a bad
   `OLAI_EMBED_SERVER` today reads as a 30-second `/health` timeout with an
   empty tail. IN: spawn, `unstartable`, drained stderr with the caller naming
   the sink, kill-with-scope (SIGTERM → grace → SIGKILL). OUT, unanimously:
   readiness, transport, restart policy, death-LOOK — "the wide `@olai/acp`
   again" otherwise. The wall converged at closing: a workspace package is a
   *wall*, not a *release*; incorporation still waits. Three deletions ride it,
   including `agent.ts`'s graceless bare `kill()` on the clean path.
2. **`meaning` joins the format floor.** `SearchHit.matched` widens to
   `optionalKey([...SEARCH_FIELDS, "meaning"])` in `format/searching.ts` —
   optionality kept, `SEARCH_FIELDS` itself untouched (a `meaning:` operator
   would be a lie the grammar then looks up); the PR's local surface schema
   dies; a claim test pins it.
3. **`searchWith` takes the parsed filter, not the typed string.** The merge
   bug the week's own receptacles caught before it shipped: unfixed, a refused
   query (`is:blocked`) would come back as a *populated* answer with no
   `refusals` key — the exact deception #168 just eliminated — with operator
   tokens embedded as prose and paraphrase hits ignoring `is:`/`file`/`under`.
   The recall leg receives the residual text; one parse, owned by the matcher.
4. **The nix sidecar convention.** `scripts/embedder.sh` is
   `scripts/acp-agent.sh` re-typed and `nix/embed.nix` confesses it; one nix
   helper + one `scripts/sidecar.sh` replace both hand-typed scripts, and the
   guard covers the script's spelling, not only the baked path. The action is
   signed three ways; vault-vs-hygiene vocabulary differs (Position C of
   2026-08-13 over again) and no position is filed.
5. **Two fences:** nothing under `packages/` outside `recall/` reads the three
   embedder env vars (gripping the read); nothing outside `recall/` spells the
   cache path or its tmp-rename. **Two watches with triggers:** `sayOnce`
   (third spelling triggers), the twice-spelled XDG convention (any third
   `olai/<face>` triggers).
6. **The stay-put list**, unopposed: the port in ops, the floor on the
   `Embedder`, cache in XDG, `null` is not an error, `recall/` in server,
   exact-first ranking, `≈` as LOOK, the sweep and socket key in `embedder.ts`,
   the linear scan as written.

## Why this debate earned its keep

Scoping to a PR sharpened rather than shrank the method: the four tests
protected the PR's own best decisions from future "tidying" (the stay-put list
is as much a finding as the receptacle), caught two merge faults that exist
only because last week's receptacles landed first, and turned the one
irreducible disagreement into a one-command experiment instead of a standoff.
The room's honesty compounded — a debater checking *her own* headline found it
false, another withdrew a claim rather than let an unmeasured assumption
stand, and the third conceded the wall in his closing when the argument
reached its target.

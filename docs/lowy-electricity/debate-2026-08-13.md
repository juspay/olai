## What happened, in plain words

Yesterday's debate sent three sockets upstream; all three shipped within a day (kolu #2158/#2159/#2160, installed by olai #129). Asked the same question cold, the room found the next layer down — and the debate's whole yield of self-corrections happened in public: fable retracted her own strongest citation and recorded her headline candidate as *failing* the oscilloscope test rather than arguing around it; grok withdrew a package he had signed one round earlier; opencode reversed himself on theme after actually reading the file he had skimmed.

## Ratified by all three (pending the human's word)

1. **`createPreference` — the headline.** The read→signal→write→watch storage circuit is hand-wired at every stored key (`layout/prefs.ts` alone spells it five times; fold memory, sidebar folders, and the done default each spell it again — the enumeration is 03.fable §I.8), and two wirings have already diverged under maintenance. One factory in `web`: codec per key, one option (`persist: false`, for the drag handles). **Theme stays OFF the circuit — unanimous by the end**: `theme/state.ts` never performs the circuit's read (the pre-module boot script owns it, because a themed first paint cannot wait for a module); folding it in costs an `apply` hook plus a read override — knobs with exactly one user. Theme keeps its own vault and imports the primitives. Claim test gripped on the **call**: no client file outside `preference.ts`, its test, and `theme/state.ts` may spell `readPreference(` / `watchPreference(`.
2. **The ask-vocabulary move.** `AskField` / `AskChoice` / `AskAnswer` / `AskOutcome` / `YES_NO` (and `FileDiff` if it is the same tenant) are ACP's elicitation shapes currently declared in `@olai/surface` — `YES_NO` is "a value neither owns" by its own author's comment. All three sign reversing the arrow so the protocol's words stop living in olai's wire spec. **Which wall receives them is Position A below.**
3. **`GitState` → `format/src/committing.ts`** beside `RepoState`; surface re-exports; the hand-kept mirrors (`surface/src/index.ts:212-218`, `ops/src/pending.ts:107-112`) die. Kitchen-sinks item 3, ratified twice now, still unshipped.
4. **Upstream: a throw must not be a white tab.** kolu's composition root takes a required `fault` the way it requires `retired`, **and the printer travels with it** — `thrown.ts`'s Safari/V8/DOMException litany is the supply side, so a required prop without the printer would leave every consumer a voltmeter. olai deletes its hand wiring (`main.tsx:66-83`) and keeps `Fault.tsx`'s LOOK. (fable's amendment, adopted by all three.)
5. **The dial URL: olai deletes `wire.ts:36-39` today** by calling the `surfaceWsUrl` helper already in the pin — the socket exists; olai just never plugged in. kolu defaulting `connectSurface.url` is worth paying **as a fix** beside `keysBus`. (Whether that default deserves the word "vault" is Position C — the actions themselves are agreed.)
6. **`scale.ts` → `theme/`** ships under **hygiene** (kitchen-sinks item 2), explicitly not counted as this debate's receptacle yield — nothing is hidden by the move.
7. **Claim tests, finally written**: the preference call-grep (above); `wire.ts`'s claim tested as `connectSurface(`, not the subpath; after the dial deletion, "nothing outside `connection/status.ts` reads the readout's raw states".
8. **`@olai/store` — recognition only, clock running.** Zero src edits across the busiest thirteen-PR week the repo has had, while `web` moved ten times. No move today; the trigger wording is Position B.
9. **Upstream fixes, not vaults**: `keysBus` O(M·N); `lifecycle_create --worktree`; the padi probe-as-evidence *leftover* of the already-paid `kolu-detect-primary` (fable mis-filed it as new; grok caught it; withdrawn).

## Killed, with the reason on record

- **`@olai/acp` as originally scoped** — its author recorded the oscilloscope reading zero (`events.ts` already hides the protocol at the module boundary) and both other debaters caught it independently. What survives is the narrowed fork below.
- **`quiet-pill-unify` as a receptacle** — a class string is not an axis; ship as cleanup. (All three, independently, unprompted.)
- **`reading.ts`/`rpc.ts` extraction** — would duplicate `events.ts`'s axis. **`trackDrag`** — variable, not volatile; a third drag is the trigger. **Splitting `@olai/format`** — the domain package's face moving with the domain is that package working. **`@olai/markdown` / `theme` / `committing` / `viewport` / `fault` / `popover`, own-repo moves, `SurfaceAppProvider` wholesale, `kolu.ts` upstream** — refused across the board.

## Three positions for the human — none forced, per grok's on-record objection

**A. Which wall receives the ask vocabulary?** *(the one live architectural cut)*
- **fable — the leaf's `./wire`**: a new workspace package `@olai/acp` (protocol files out of `chat`; `UsageFailure` translated at the seam, not moved); a manifest makes the boundary machine-checked where today a comment checks it; the precedent read as "the package that *speaks* the foreign thing owns its words" (`RepoState` declared by `@olai/git`, format re-exports).
- **opencode + grok — the format floor** (`format/src/ask.ts`), this cycle: the tree already built the floor for cross-face vocabulary (`failure.ts`'s own header; `RepoState` installed, `GitState` owed — ask types the third tenant); no new manifest; the precedent read as "leaf owns *subprocess*, floor owns *words*" — ask types have no subprocess. A plumbing-only leaf stays signable later, after the floor move and once `mcp-fail-visible`'s in-flight traffic settles.
- The crux in one sentence (fable's, adopted by the room): **is `@olai/format` the floor for anything two faces share, or the domain package that re-exports what the leaves own?**

**B. The store-graduation trigger — two wordings (down from three; fable withdrew her second-face conjunct on the article's own argument):**
- **fable + grok — stillness alone**: unedited across two consecutive releases (or 180 days tagless, counted from 2026-08-12); a FileSystem face landing against the unchanged socket *closes the window early*; a second consumer is neither necessary nor sufficient.
- **opencode — stillness (90 days) AND a second `Store.make` consumer**, held as a kept position with the honest rider that if stillness runs the full 90 with `web` churning at this rate, "stillness alone will have been the right wording."

**C. The word for kolu defaulting `connectSurface.url`:** opencode — a vault (the strongest small one); fable + grok — a fix, since `surfaceWsUrl` already encapsulates the volatility. Both dispositions produce the identical two PRs; only the ledger's vocabulary differs.

## Why this debate earned its keep

The unbiased two-sentence charter reproduced the roadmap's own parked questions without being shown them — `preference-receptacle` arrived as the headline from two altitudes independently, and `quiet-pill-unify` was rejected three ways for the same reason — and then went further: it found the theme exclusion no one had filed, killed its own strongest package candidate on a failed test its author reported against herself, and left exactly one architectural question standing, stated narrowly enough to decide without re-reading three briefs.

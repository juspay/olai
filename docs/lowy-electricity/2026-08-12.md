| | |
|---|---|
| **The question** | Argue FOR volatility-based extraction: what in olai deserves a "receptacle" — a stable socket hiding enormous churn, like a wall outlet hides AC/DC, voltage, phase, and source from every appliance? |
| **The frame** | Juval Löwy's *Righting Software* ch. 2 ([the article](https://www.informit.com/articles/article.aspx?p=2995357&seqNum=2)), read in full by every debater. Kolu's own surface framework came out of this thinking. |

## What happened, in plain words

Everyone argued *for* extraction — and still landed on **zero new olai packages**. Not because extraction lost, but because the debate found the receptacles are mostly already installed. What's missing is on the *other* sides of olai:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The three real extractions are upstream, in kolu:**
> 1. **Finish the build helper** (`buildSurfaceClient` gains `precompress` + `splitting` as ONE socket) so every app stops hand-rolling asset compression and code-splitting — olai then *deletes* its two workaround files (`precompress.ts`, most of `markdown.ts`); the deletion is the installation.
> 2. **Make the connection light unable to lie** — today the "connected" dot can be green while data has silently stopped arriving, unless the app remembers to double-check `client.health()`. The framework should own that truth (status may not say `live` over an errored subscription; pending does not degrade); olai keeps its own wording (`status.ts`'s LOOK).
> 3. **Take the file-chunking arithmetic** (`chunkBase64` + budget helpers, beside `RPC_MAX_FRAME_BYTES`) that stops a big upload from killing the whole tab — every app that uploads will need it; it was already copied from kolu's padi once. The picture gate stays in olai.

Inside olai, the debate ratified two things:

- **`@olai/store` — the package that watches your files — is already the textbook receptacle** (its consumers never see a file-watcher event, a timestamp trick, or a rename dance). Moving it to its own repo waits for a measurable trigger: its public face going untouched for a defined quiet period. Three trigger wordings were recorded for the human to pick: **fable** — stillness (two release windows) AND a second FileSystem face landing; **opencode** — stillness (90 days) AND any second consumer of `Store.make`; **grok** — stillness alone (180 days, tagless stand-in), a FileSystem face landing against the unchanged socket *closes the window early*, and a second consumer is neither necessary nor sufficient (registered objection: requiring one would re-litigate Löwy — reuse is a consequence, the still plug is the product).
- **Module-level sockets get "claim tests"** — tiny automated checks that pin promises like "only one file in the app knows a websocket exists," because such promises are otherwise just comments, and comments rot (both debates caught them rotting).

Killed on the way, with the reason on record: `@olai/markdown` (the consumers plug the house's dialect — served-directory pictures, tag pills, per-page footnote ids — not generic rendering; the security boundary is already one file, `sanitise.ts`); `@olai/theme` ("adding a theme is adding a row" *is* the encapsulation); a `disk.ts` promotion (only `store.ts`/`probe.ts` import it — ops and server plug the store); `changesOf` extraction ("in olai's words" — the format's own difference vocabulary).

## Why this debate earned its keep

The prior kitchen-sinks debate *prosecuted* a list of proposed extractions down to zero packages. This one argued the opposite direction — extract everything the analogy justifies! — and reached the same zero, plus three upstream findings the first debate never saw. Debaters caught and withdrew their own factual errors mid-debate by re-reading the code, and the empirical anchor held: `@olai/git`'s socket has never been edited since its extraction while the policy file above it changed in every git PR since. That is what a good receptacle looks like — and the debate's yield is completing the two half-sockets consumers still bring a voltmeter to, plus one vault olai should stop threatening to incorporate before its plug sits still.

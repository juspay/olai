# Theme

Theme decides what olai looks like: the palette, the typeface and the size of everything.[^rows]

## Theme

Four palettes, drawn as swatches in the colours they set: `chalk` and `reef` are light, `basalt` and `abyss` are dark.[^swatch] Ahead of them stands **Follow the system**, where a browser that has picked nothing starts.[^system] Tab reaches the swatches and `Space` or `Enter` picks one.[^open] The palette paints more than the page.[^paints]

## Font

A select of olai's typeface catalogue, in three groups — **olai**, **Generic** and **Typefaces**. The default, `olai`, sets titles and documents in Literata, the interface in Inter and code in iA Writer Mono.[^select]

## Size

One number sets the whole page, furniture and type together.[^layout]

| Choice | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Medium** | your browser's own text size |
| **Large** | one notch up, and olai's default |
| **Larger** | two notches up |

## What is restored, and what is forgotten

Your palette, face and size are put back before the page is first drawn, so nothing flashes in one palette and settles in another.[^forgotten]

## When the screen is not olai's to paint

Three system settings arrive without passing through these rows, and olai follows all three live, with nothing to reload: high contrast,[^contrast] more contrast[^more] and less movement.[^motion]

[^rows]: The three rows, **Theme**, **Font** and **Size**, are the **Appearance** group at the top of the preferences panel. The choices are this browser's, carried to its other tabs and never sent to the server.

[^swatch]: A swatch carries no words, so its name is its accessible name and the tip a pointer gets. The palette in force is named once beside the row's label.

[^system]: The system's own light or dark setting chooses between `chalk` and `basalt`, and the label reads `System · Chalk`. Pick a swatch and that pick wins over the system until you press **Follow the system** again, and the label reads the bare name, `Chalk`.

[^open]: The panel stays open, because a palette is judged on the page it paints.

[^paints]: The palette also sets the colour a phone draws its status bar in, the mark in the browser tab, and the icon. When an agent is waiting on you, the tab's name takes a `●` in front of it and the icon is marked to match.

[^select]: The closed select is drawn in the face in force. The options are not: a browser asked to draw twenty names in twenty faces would fetch every one of them to open the list.

[^layout]: The line between the phone layout and the desktop one does not move with the size, so asking for larger type gives you larger type rather than a laptop drawn as a phone.

[^forgotten]: A stored choice naming a palette, face or size olai does not offer is forgotten: the face and the size go back to their defaults, and the palette goes back to following the system. Where a browser allows no storage at all, a pick still works and lasts until the page is closed.

[^contrast]: Asked for high contrast, a browser throws olai's palette away and repaints from the reader's own, deleting every shadow on the way. The keyboard's focus ring survives, because it is an outline rather than a shadow.

[^more]: Asked for more contrast, the overflow bar, which is deliberately quiet, draws its thumb at the full ink of whichever palette is in force.

[^motion]: Asked for less movement, olai stops moving: switches and chevrons land in their new state rather than travelling to it, and live cues hold still. A reveal puts the row on screen rather than gliding to it. Colours still fade, and nothing a cue was saying is lost with its movement.

<details>
<summary>What a system colour repaints, and what happens when a row is off</summary>

Anything that said its state in a colour alone says it again in the system's colours: a switch's two positions, the segment of a strip in force, the selected tab of a ledger. So do the round marks for an agent's standing, a live turn and a transaction. The **Theme** swatches are the single exception, on purpose: a palette preview with its colours taken has nothing left to say.

| What is off | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| theme | no **Appearance** rows and no tab mark; the rest of the preferences panel stands |
| preferences, the sidebar or the layout | the palette, face and size keep working and keep following storage |

</details>

> **`@olai/store` graduates to its own repo when (a) `packages/store/src/index.ts` has gone 90 days unedited, AND (b) a second consumer of `Store.make` exists — either the worker/browser face the store's own dev note anticipates, or any surface-app outside this house. This is fable's socket-stability test and grok's "aged at one consumer" rule composed with the article's refusal to make reuse a precondition: the date does the waiting the corporate-structure argument conflated.
